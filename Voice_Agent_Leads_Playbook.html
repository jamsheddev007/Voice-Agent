    Chapter 01 · The answer first

    Target order and the 30-day lead plan

    Go after three waves, one at a time. Don't start Wave 2 until Wave 1 has 3 paying clients and a case study.

    Wave 1 · now · months 1–3

    Freight brokers and carriers

    Our edge: we know the work, our own dispatch is the demo, and we have a network. Bigger deals ($3k–$6k setup + $1k–$2k/mo), B2B buyers and calls between businesses.

    Sell: check-call agent, after-hours carrier line, capacity calls.

    Wave 2 · months 3–6

    Home services: HVAC, plumbing, roofing, restoration

    Hundreds of thousands of firms, missed calls cost them real jobs, owners decide fast. Smaller deals ($1.5k + $300–$800/mo), so sell a fixed package.

    Sell: AI receptionist + emergency routing + booking.

    Wave 3 · month 6+

    Property management and personal-injury law

    High value per call (a tenant emergency, a new injury case). More process and rules, so go in with case studies.

    Sell: maintenance line, after-hours intake. Dental/clinics only once we have a HIPAA BAA setup.

    Chapter 02 · The market

    Why now, and how big

    Big companies already run voice AI. Small and mid-size firms are only starting, and they miss the most calls. That is our market.

    Our serviceable market, simply

    Who has the problem

    Any business where the phone brings in money or moves work: freight, trades, property, legal intake, clinics.

    Who can pay

    Firms with 5–200 staff and at least ~$1M revenue. Below that, owners won't spend $300+/mo; above that, IT teams build it themselves.

    Who we can reach

    US first (biggest, cold email allowed), then the UK (best time-zone fit from Lahore, stricter consent rules).

    Size your own list in 10 minutesDon't trust report totals. Run the Apollo and Google Maps searches in chapter 06 for one sector and one region. The count of firms that match is your real market for the next 90 days.

    Chapter 04 · The people

    Who signs, who uses it, who blocks it

    Always reach the owner or the head of operations in firms under 100 staff. They feel the missed calls and can say yes on one call.

    Freight brokerage owner / President

    Titles
    Owner, President, CEO, Founder
    Cares about
    Margin per load, covering loads fast, not hiring more dispatchers
    Pain words
    "check calls", "after hours", "can't find carriers", "turnover"
    Hook
    "Your dispatchers spend 4–6 hours a day asking 'where are you?'"

    VP / Director of Operations (freight)

    Titles
    VP Operations, Director of Operations, Operations Manager, Head of Carrier Sales
    Cares about
    On-time updates, customer complaints, team workload
    Role
    Champion: runs the pilot, gives you the TMS access
    Hook
    A 60-second recording of a check call that updates the TMS

    Carrier owner / Dispatch manager

    Titles
    Owner, Fleet Manager, Dispatch Manager, Safety & Compliance
    Cares about
    Loaded miles, driver retention, answering brokers fast
    Pain words
    "brokers keep calling", "nights and weekends", "driver updates"
    Hook
    "Answer every broker call 24/7, even at 2 am."

    Home-services owner

    Titles
    Owner, President, General Manager
    Cares about
    Booked jobs, not losing emergency calls to competitors
    Pain words
    "missed calls", "answering service", "after-hours", "CSR quit"
    Hook
    "I called you at 7:12 pm on Tuesday and got voicemail."

    Office manager / CSR lead

    Titles
    Office Manager, Customer Service Manager, Dispatcher, Call Center Manager
    Role
    User and possible blocker: fears replacement
    Answer
    "It takes the night calls and overflow, not your job. You get booked jobs and summaries."

    Property manager / PI law intake

    Titles
    Property Manager, Director of Property Management, Managing Partner, Intake Manager
    Cares about
    Tenant emergencies at night; signing new cases before competitors
    Hook
    "Every after-hours call answered, triaged, and sent to on-call in 60 seconds."

      Chapter 08 · Prospecting rules

      Stay legal while finding leads

      General information, not legal advice.

      RuleWhat it meansWhat we do
      CAN-SPAM (US email)Cold B2B email is allowed with honest subject lines, a real postal address and a working opt-outEvery email has company name, address and "reply no to stop"; opt-outs removed within days
      TCPA + FCC AI ruling (US calls)AI/prerecorded voice calls need prior consent; autodialed calls and texts to mobiles are restrictedNever use our own voice agent for cold calls. People make cold calls by hand; texts only after they reply
      Do Not Call listsSmall-business owners often list their mobile numbers, which can be on the National DNCScrub mobile numbers against the DNC; prefer main business lines
      UK PECRB2B email to corporate addresses is allowed with opt-out; sole traders and partnerships need consent. B2B cold calls are allowed unless the number is on TPS/CTPSScreen against TPS/CTPS; don't email sole traders without consent
      UK GDPR / GDPRBusiness contact data is still personal data; you need a legitimate interest and a privacy noticeShort privacy notice link in emails; delete data on request
      Data source termsLinkedIn and some sites ban scrapingUse Apollo, Sales Navigator, public government data and official exports, not scrapers that break terms
      Missed-call testCalling a business line once like a customer is normalNever leave fake messages or book fake jobs; say who you are if someone answers
